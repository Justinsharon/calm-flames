India's botanical heritage, in wax

Long before scented candles, Indian homes were perfumed by what grew nearby: sandalwood paste in temples, jasmine strung into evening garlands, vetiver mats cooled with water through the summer. Our candles start from those same plants.

"A scent can carry you home faster than any photograph."

Each blend is built the way an attar maker would: a bright top note to greet you, a floral heart, and a slow woody base that lingers after the flame is out.
