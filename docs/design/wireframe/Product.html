Description
Each Royal Urli is poured by hand in small batches in our studio. The lotus is shaped petal by petal from a blend of soy and coconut wax, then scented with pure mogra and rose oils over a soft sandalwood base. Float it in water with a few petals, or let it stand on its own as a centrepiece.
How to burn & care
  • Burn for 2–3 hours at a time so the wax melts evenly.
  • Trim the wick to 5 mm before every burn.
  • Keep away from drafts, children and pets. Never leave a flame unattended.
Ingredients
Natural soy and coconut wax · mogra, rose and sandalwood essential oils · cotton wick · brass-finish urli. No paraffin, no synthetic dyes.

Packed within 2 days, delivered in 3–7 days with tracking on WhatsApp. Unopened candles can be returned within 7 days.
