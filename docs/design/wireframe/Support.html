FAQ

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Our studio team replies on WhatsApp within a few hours.

Where is my order?
Use Track your order above with your order number and phone. You also get a tracking link on WhatsApp as soon as it ships.
My candle arrived damaged. What now?
Sorry about that. Send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery on WhatsApp or the form below and we'll send a replacement at no cost.
Can I change my address after ordering?
Yes, until the order is packed (usually within 24 hours). Message us on WhatsApp with your order number.
How do refunds work?
Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 working days after we receive the return. COD refunds go to your UPI ID.
Do you ship outside India?
Not yet. We currently deliver to all serviceable pincodes across India.
Can I add a gift message?
Yes. Add it in the cart or in Build a Box and we'll handwrite it on a card.
